# DeepTrace

## AI-Powered Digital Media Forensics Platform

DeepTrace is an AI-powered digital media forensics platform designed to analyze digital content and identify potential signs of manipulation, synthetic generation, deepfakes, and AI-generated media.

The platform supports **image, video, and audio analysis** through a unified interface and presents the results using an interpretable forensic assessment system. DeepTrace combines detection outputs, metadata analysis, artifact analysis, temporal analysis, and evidence signals to generate a clear assessment of uploaded media.

Rather than providing only a binary result such as **Real** or **Fake**, DeepTrace provides a detailed forensic assessment containing:

- Media classification
- Detection confidence
- Trust score
- Risk level
- Evidence signals
- Metadata findings
- Suspicious regions or segments
- Analysis history
- Downloadable forensic PDF reports

---

## Disclaimer

DeepTrace is designed as an AI-powered digital media forensic assistance platform.

The results generated by the system are based on the available analysis pipelines, detection models, and forensic signals. A classification, confidence score, trust score, or risk level should not be considered absolute proof of authenticity, manipulation, synthetic generation, or deepfake activity.

The platform is intended to provide decision-support information and assist users in identifying media that may require additional investigation.

For high-stakes use cases, DeepTrace results should be evaluated alongside expert human review and additional forensic methods.

# Unified Forensic Assessment

Every media analysis produces a standardized forensic assessment. This ensures that image, video, and audio analyses follow a consistent result format throughout the platform.

The assessment includes the following major components.

## Classification

Classification describes the primary forensic conclusion generated by the analysis system.

Examples include:

- Likely Authentic
- Likely AI Generated
- Likely Manipulated
- Likely Voice Clone
- Manipulated Segments Detected

The classification is generated based on the combined output of the available detection and analysis modules.

---

## Confidence Score

The confidence score represents how strongly the analysis pipeline supports the generated classification.

For example:

**Confidence: 91.5%**

A higher confidence value indicates stronger agreement between the detection signals and the final classification.

Confidence should be interpreted alongside the available evidence rather than as an independent guarantee of authenticity or manipulation.

---

## Trust Score

The trust score provides an estimate of how likely the analyzed media is to be considered authentic based on the forensic signals returned by the analysis pipeline.

The score ranges from:

**0 → Low Trust**

**100 → High Trust**

A lower trust score indicates stronger signs of manipulation, synthetic generation, or suspicious activity, while a higher trust score indicates fewer detected forensic irregularities.

---

## Risk Level

DeepTrace categorizes analyzed media into three risk levels.

| Risk Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 LOW | Low indication of manipulation or synthetic generation |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | Some suspicious signals detected and further review may be required |
| 🔴 HIGH | Strong indication of manipulation, synthetic generation, spoofing, or deepfake activity |

The risk level provides a quick interpretation of the overall forensic assessment.

---

# Evidence-Based Analysis

Instead of displaying only a final classification, DeepTrace provides evidence explaining why a piece of media was flagged.

Each evidence signal contains:

- Detection source
- Detection score
- Explanation of the finding

For example:

**Source:** AI Detector

**Score:** 91%

**Finding:** Synthetic generation patterns detected in high-frequency texture regions.

Different analysis modules can contribute evidence to the final forensic assessment.

Possible evidence sources include:

- AI Detection Models
- Artifact Analysis
- Metadata Analysis
- Frame-Level Detection
- Temporal Consistency Analysis
- Spoof Detection
- Spectral Analysis
- Prosody Analysis

This evidence-based approach improves the interpretability of the system and helps users understand the signals that contributed to the final assessment.

# Analysis Workflow

The general DeepTrace analysis workflow follows the steps below:

1. The user selects a media type.
2. The user uploads an image, video, or audio file.
3. The system validates the uploaded media.
4. The appropriate analysis pipeline processes the file.
5. Detection modules generate forensic signals.
6. Supporting evidence is collected.
7. The system calculates the confidence score, trust score, and risk level.
8. A final classification is generated.
9. The results are displayed through the unified results interface.
10. The completed analysis is saved to persistent history.
11. The user can generate a forensic PDF report.
12. The dashboard updates to reflect the new analysis.

This workflow provides a consistent experience across all supported media types.
